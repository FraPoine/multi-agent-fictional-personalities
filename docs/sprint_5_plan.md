# Sprint 5 Plan

## Objective

Build from the mock-only evaluation foundation toward a genuine, controlled
Sherlock Holmes/Hercule Poirot pilot without treating synthetic fixtures as
research evidence.

## Implemented foundation

- mock-only technical trial generation with one trial per character per topic;
- provenance-free public trials and private ground truth/traceability;
- separate local rater-interface dry run;
- validated genuine-response persistence;
- separate synthetic development checks;
- reproducible basic analysis with a 50% baseline and Wilson interval.

## Still pending

- a verified live provider and genuinely generated, diverse messages;
- token and cost measurements from real provider usage;
- the real two-character pilot and genuine human responses;
- scientifically interpretable analysis and limitations reporting;
- L, Professor Layton, and the final four-character study.

Sprint 5 must not be marked complete until the scoped live-provider and real
pilot work has been implemented, verified, and documented.
