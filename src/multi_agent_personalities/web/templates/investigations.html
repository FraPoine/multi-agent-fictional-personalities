How to investigate

  1. Read the case opening together.
  2. Choose a lead to visit when lead controls become available.
  3. The Game Master reveals information explicitly; known information remains available.
  4. Returning to a lead creates a new chronological visit.
  5. Conclude only when the group is ready.

This generic guide does not reproduce a commercial rulebook.
