How to investigate

  1. Select a lead to read its complete semantic history.
  2. Historical leads remain readable; revisit explicitly to continue.
  3. Revealed information persists across later visits and discussions.
  4. Generate another bounded investigator exchange when needed.
  5. Presenting the Final Theory ends the investigation and makes it read-only.

This is generic local guidance, not a reproduction of a commercial rulebook.
